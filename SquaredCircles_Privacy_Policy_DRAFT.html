Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Overview

Squared Circles is an Android arcade game published by DuckedGames. We aim to minimise personal data collection. We do not sell players’ personal information. This policy describes the currently planned Android test build and its integrated services.

1. Information and game saves

The game stores levels, high scores, virtual Squares, XP, cosmetics, achievements, inventory and preferences on your device. You can play without creating a DuckedGames account or providing your name, email address or phone number.

The current build is not yet connected to a working Google Play Games cloud-saving service. If we add optional Google Play Games sign-in and cloud saves, we will update this policy before enabling the feature.

2. Advertising

The Android game integrates Google AdMob for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. The current development build uses Google test advertisements, which do not generate advertising revenue.

Google’s advertising software may process information including IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers, where permitted, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. This information is processed by Google according to its policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google’s explanation of information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not independently sell or trade players’ personal data to advertisers.

3. Children and families

Squared Circles is intended to be suitable for a broad audience, including children under 13. The game does not ask children to provide their name, email address or phone number to play.

We intend to configure advertising, age treatment and privacy controls in line with applicable children’s privacy rules and Google Play Families requirements before making the game publicly available. Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy questions using the email below.

4. Consent and privacy choices

The Android game includes Google privacy-consent functionality. Depending on location, age treatment and applicable requirements, Google may display privacy choices, or no form may be shown. Where Google makes privacy options available, they can be accessed through the game’s settings.

Core gameplay does not require agreeing to optional personalised advertising. Optional ad-based rewards may be unavailable if ads cannot be requested or loaded.

5. Purchases

The current Android test build does not process real-money purchases. No Ads and other paid features are not live yet. If purchases are launched through Google Play Billing, we will update this policy to explain purchase verification and restoration.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game progress is stored locally on the device. You can clear local game progress through Android app storage settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to any backup or restore settings on your device.

Google independently processes and retains information collected by its services under Google’s applicable policies. To ask about data associated with Squared Circles or your privacy rights, contact DuckedGames at the email below. Cloud save deletion information will be added before cloud saving launches.

7. International privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you or your parent/guardian rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal data, to object to some processing, or to withdraw consent where applicable. Contact us to make a request. You may also manage information held by Google using Google’s privacy controls.

8. Updates

We may revise this policy if the game, advertising systems or storage features change. We will update the date at the top when we publish a revised version.
